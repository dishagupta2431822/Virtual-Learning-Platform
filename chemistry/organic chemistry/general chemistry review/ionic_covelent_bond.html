Lab Manual

Objective

Classify unknown substances as Ionic or Covalent through thermal stability, solvent dissolution, and electrical conductivity testing.

Chemical Bonding Reference:

  • Ionic Compounds: High melting point ($>300^\circ\text{C}$), soluble in water, free ions conduct electricity in aqueous form.
  • Covalent Compounds: Low melting point ($<300^\circ\text{C}$), non-conductive (neutral molecules).
