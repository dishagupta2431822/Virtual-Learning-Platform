In H2, two 1s atomic orbitals combine to form a lower-energy bonding sigma molecular orbital ($\sigma_{1s}$) and a higher-energy antibonding molecular orbital ($\sigma^*_{1s}$). Both electrons occupy the bonding orbital, yielding a bond order of 1.
