In a coffee-cup calorimeter operating at constant pressure, heat lost by the hot metal equals heat gained by the water ($q_{\text{metal}} + q_{\text{water}} = 0$). By measuring the final equilibrium temperature, we can determine unknown specific heat capacities or reaction enthalpies.
