In an octahedral coordination complex, the six ligands cause electrostatic repulsion on the metal d-orbitals, splitting them into lower energy t2g and higher energy eg sets. Electronic transitions between these d-orbitals absorb specific wavelengths of visible light, imparting characteristic colors to transition metal solutions.
