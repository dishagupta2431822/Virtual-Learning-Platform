Lactic acid possesses a central carbon atom bonded to four different groups: a methyl group (-CH3), a hydroxyl group (-OH), a carboxylic acid group (-COOH), and a hydrogen atom (-H). It exists as a pair of optical enantiomers.

Enantiomeric Relationship:

Enantiomers are non-superimposable mirror images of each other. They share identical physical properties except for the direction in which they rotate plane-polarized light.
