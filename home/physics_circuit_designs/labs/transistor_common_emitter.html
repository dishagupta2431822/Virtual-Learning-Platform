Tutorial & Theory

Common-Emitter Amplification

The Common-Emitter (CE) Amplifier provides high voltage and current gain, making it the most widely used BJT configuration in analog circuit design.

Phase Inversion

The output voltage at the collector is 180° out of phase with the input voltage applied at the base.

Q-Point Biasing

Proper biasing ensures the quiescent operating point ($V_{CEQ}, I_{CQ}$) sits safely in the active linear region, preventing clipping distortion.

Experiment Objectives

  • Calculate voltage gain $A_v$ across varying collector resistors $R_c$.
  • Analyze DC load-line limits (saturation and cut-off points).
  • Observe 180° signal phase inversion on the oscilloscope.
