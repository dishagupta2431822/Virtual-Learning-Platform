Tutorial & Theory

Simple Harmonic Motion

A simple pendulum consists of a mass $m$ suspended from a frictionless pivot by a light, inextensible string of length $L$.

Small-Angle Approximation

For small angular displacements ($\theta \le 15^\circ$), $\sin\theta \approx \theta$, yielding simple harmonic motion with period $T = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{L}{g}}$.

Mass Independence

The oscillation period depends only on length $L$ and acceleration due to gravity $g$, completely independent of the bob's mass $m$.

Experiment Objectives

  • Measure period $T$ vs string length $L$ and plot $T^2$ vs $L$.
  • Determine local gravitational acceleration $g$ from pendulum period data.
  • Verify that mass $m$ does not influence the oscillation period.
