Tutorial & Theory

Mutual Induction & Transformers

A transformer is an electrical device that transfers electrical energy between two or more circuits through electromagnetic mutual induction.

Turns Ratio Equation

The ratio of secondary voltage to primary voltage equals the ratio of secondary turns to primary turns: $\frac{V_s}{V_p} = \frac{N_s}{N_p}$.

Step-Up vs Step-Down

If $N_s > N_p$, voltage is stepped up ($V_s > V_p$). If $N_s < N_p$, voltage is stepped down ($V_s < V_p$).

Experiment Objectives

  • Verify transformer voltage ratios across varying turn counts ($N_s$ and $N_p$).
  • Distinguish between step-up and step-down power distribution setups.
  • Observe mutual magnetic flux linking primary and secondary coils.
