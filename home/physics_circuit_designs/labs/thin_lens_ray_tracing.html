Tutorial & Theory

Thin Lens Equation

A thin lens is a lens with a thickness that is negligible compared to the radii of curvature of its surfaces. Lenses form images by refracting incoming light rays.

Convex (Converging) Lenses

Convex lenses have positive focal lengths ($+f$) and typically form real, inverted images when the object is beyond the focal point ($d_o > f$).

Concave (Diverging) Lenses

Concave lenses have negative focal lengths ($-f$) and always form virtual, upright, and diminished images regardless of object position.

Experiment Objectives

  • Verify thin lens formula across varying object distances ($d_o$).
  • Distinguish between real inverted images and virtual upright magnifications.
  • Calculate linear magnification ($m = h_i / h_o$).
