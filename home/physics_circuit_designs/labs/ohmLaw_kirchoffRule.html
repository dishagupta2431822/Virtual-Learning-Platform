Tutorial & Theory

Ohm's & Kirchhoff's Laws

Ohm's Law states that the current ($I$) through a conductor between two points is directly proportional to the voltage ($V$) across the two points ($V = IR$).

Kirchhoff's Current Law (KCL)

The algebraic sum of all currents entering any node (junction) in a circuit must equal zero ($\sum I = 0$).

Kirchhoff's Voltage Law (KVL)

The directed sum of electrical potential differences (voltage drops and rises) around any closed loop is zero ($\sum V = 0$).

Experiment Objectives

  • Measure nodal voltages across series-parallel resistor networks.
  • Verify Kirchhoff's Current Law at parallel junction nodes.
  • Verify Kirchhoff's Voltage Law across closed circuit loops.
