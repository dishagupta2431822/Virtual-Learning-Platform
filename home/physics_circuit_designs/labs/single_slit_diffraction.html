Tutorial & Theory

Single Slit Diffraction

When monochromatic light passes through a narrow single slit of width comparable to its wavelength, light bends around edges and interferes, producing a broad central bright maximum flanked by dimmer secondary maxima.

Diffraction Minima Formula

Secondary minima occur when $a \sin\theta = m\lambda$, where $m = \pm 1, \pm 2, \dots$

Central Maximum Width

The total width of the bright central maximum is exactly twice the distance to the first minimum: $w = \frac{2\lambda L}{a}$.

Experiment Objectives

  • Observe central maximum broadening as slit width ($a$) decreases.
  • Calculate angular position ($\theta_1$) of secondary diffraction minima.
  • Verify sinc-squared intensity distribution profile.
