Tutorial & Theory

Null-Deflection Measurements

A Wheatstone Bridge is an electrical circuit used to measure an unknown electrical resistance by balancing two legs of a bridge circuit.

Condition for Balance

When the galvanometer shows zero deflection ($I_g = 0$), the potential difference across the detector is zero, yielding $\frac{R_1}{R_2} = \frac{R_3}{R_x}$.

High Precision

Because null measurements do not depend on absolute meter calibrations, Wheatstone bridges offer exceptionally high precision.

Experiment Objectives

  • Determine unknown resistor value $R_x$ using null-deflection balancing.
  • Verify bridge balance equation across multiple ratio arms.
  • Calculate measurement percentage error against nominal values.
