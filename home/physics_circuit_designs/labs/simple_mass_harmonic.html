Tutorial & Theory

Hooke's Law & Energy

Hooke's Law states that the restorative force exerted by an ideal spring is directly proportional to its displacement from equilibrium: $F = -kx$.

Oscillation Period ($T$)

The period of a spring-mass system depends on mass $m$ and spring constant $k$: $T = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{m}{k}}$.

Elastic Potential Energy

Energy stored in a stretched or compressed spring is given by $U = \frac{1}{2}kx^2$, which continuously exchanges with kinetic energy.

Experiment Objectives

  • Verify oscillation period dependency on spring stiffness ($k$) and mass ($m$).
  • Calculate elastic potential energy and monitor energy conservation.
  • Observe amplitude decay profiles under light and heavy damping coefficients.
