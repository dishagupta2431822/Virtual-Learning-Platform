Tutorial & Theory

Snell's Law of Refraction

When light travels from one transparent medium into another, its speed changes, causing the light ray to bend at the boundary. This phenomenon is known as refraction.

Snell's Law

The relationship between angles of incidence and refraction is given by $n_1 \sin\theta_1 = n_2 \sin\theta_2$.

Total Internal Reflection (TIR)

When light travels from a denser medium to a rarer medium ($n_1 > n_2$) at an angle exceeding the Critical Angle ($\theta_c$), all light is reflected back internally.

Experiment Objectives

  • Verify Snell's Law across varying refractive indices.
  • Calculate and observe the critical angle for glass-to-air boundaries.
  • Analyze total internal reflection (TIR) conditions.
