Tutorial & Theory

RC Circuit Filtering

An RC filter is an electronic circuit composed of resistors and capacitors that passes signals of certain frequencies while attenuating others.

Cutoff Frequency ($f_c$)

The frequency at which the power drops to half its passband value (-3 dB point): $f_c = \frac{1}{2\pi RC}$.

Bode Magnitude Plot

A logarithmic graph displaying filter gain in decibels ($20 \log_{10} \vert{}H(f)\vert{}$) versus frequency.

Experiment Objectives

  • Measure cutoff frequency ($f_c$) and verify -3 dB attenuation.
  • Construct and analyze Bode magnitude plots for low-pass and high-pass filters.
  • Examine phase shift variations across frequency decades.
