Tutorial & Theory

AC-to-DC Rectification

Rectification is the process of converting alternating current (AC), which periodically reverses direction, into direct current (DC), which flows in only one direction.

Bridge Rectifier Advantage

A full-wave bridge rectifier utilizes 4 diodes to invert the negative half-cycles of AC, doubling ripple frequency ($100\text{ Hz}$ or $120\text{ Hz}$) and significantly improving DC output quality.

Smoothing Capacitor Filter

Adding a parallel capacitor charges up to peak voltage and discharges slowly through the load resistor, smoothing out voltage ripples into steady DC.

Experiment Objectives

  • Compare half-wave and full-wave bridge rectified waveforms.
  • Measure peak-to-peak ripple voltage across varying smoothing capacitors.
  • Calculate DC voltage regulation and ripple factors.
