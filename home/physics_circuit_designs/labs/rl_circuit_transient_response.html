Tutorial & Theory

Inductive Transient Response

When a switch is closed in a circuit containing a resistor and an inductor, the inductor opposes changes in current due to self-induction (Faraday's Law).

Time Constant (τ = L/R)

One time constant (τ) is the duration required for current to reach 63.2% of its maximum steady-state value ($I_{max} = V_0/R$).

Inductive Kickback

When the switch is opened abruptly, $\frac{di}{dt}$ becomes extremely high, producing a massive voltage spike ($V = L\frac{di}{dt}$) across terminals.

Experiment Objectives

  • Calculate time constant τ for different inductance and resistance pairs.
  • Observe magnetic field energy storage ($U_B = \frac{1}{2} Li^2$) during current growth.
  • Analyze current checkpoints at $1\tau, 2\tau, 3\tau,$ and $5\tau$.
