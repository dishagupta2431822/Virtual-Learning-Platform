Tutorial & Theory

Operational Amplifiers

An Operational Amplifier (Op-Amp) is a high-gain electronic voltage amplifier with differential inputs. Using negative feedback, precise linear gain blocks are created.

Virtual Ground Concept

Due to extremely high open-loop gain and negative feedback, the inverting terminal ($V-$) is held at nearly 0V (Virtual Ground).

Inverting vs. Non-Inverting

Inverting amplifiers invert signal phase by $180^\circ$ ($Av = -\frac{Rf}{Rin}$), whereas non-inverting amplifiers maintain phase alignment with higher input impedance ($Av = 1 + \frac{Rf}{Rin}$).

Experiment Objectives

  • Build inverting gain blocks and verify virtual ground behavior.
  • Switch to non-inverting mode and observe phase alignment.
  • Analyze rail-to-rail output voltage saturation (clipping).
