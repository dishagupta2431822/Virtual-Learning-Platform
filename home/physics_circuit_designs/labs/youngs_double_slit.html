Tutorial & Theory

Wave Interference Principles

In Young's Double Slit Experiment, coherent light passing through two narrow slits interferes constructively and destructively, creating alternating bright and dark fringes on a screen.

Constructive Interference

Bright fringes occur when the path difference between waves is an integer multiple of wavelength ($d \sin\theta = m\lambda$).

Destructive Interference

Dark fringes occur when path difference is a half-integer multiple of wavelength ($d \sin\theta = (m + \frac{1}{2})\lambda$).

Experiment Objectives

  • Observe alternating bright and dark fringe interference patterns.
  • Analyze the effect of laser wavelength (λ) on fringe spacing (Δy).
  • Verify slit separation ($d$) and screen distance ($L$) dependency.
