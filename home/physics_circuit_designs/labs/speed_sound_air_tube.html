Tutorial & Theory

Acoustic Resonance

When sound waves travel inside a pipe or air column, reflections at the boundaries form standing sound waves through constructive interference at specific resonant frequencies.

Speed of Sound in Air

The velocity of sound depends on air temperature $T$ (°C): $v \approx 331.4 + 0.6T\text{ m/s}$.

Closed vs Open Pipes

A closed pipe has a displacement node at the closed end and supports only odd harmonics ($f_n = \frac{nv}{4L}$). An open pipe supports all harmonics ($f_n = \frac{nv}{2L}$).

Experiment Objectives

  • Measure speed of sound variations with air temperature ($T$).
  • Compare acoustic resonance in closed-end vs open-open pipes.
  • Identify acoustic pressure nodes and antinodes across harmonic modes.
