Tutorial & Theory

Capacitive Transient Response

When a DC voltage is applied to a resistor-capacitor (RC) circuit, the capacitor does not charge instantaneously. The voltage builds up exponentially over time.

Time Constant (τ = RC)

One time constant (τ) is the time required for the capacitor to charge to approximately 63.2% of its maximum voltage source.

Steady State (5τ)

By 5 time constants ($t = 5\tau$), the capacitor is considered 99.3% fully charged, and circuit current drops to zero.

Experiment Objectives

  • Calculate time constant τ for varying resistor and capacitor values.
  • Observe exponential charging and discharging curves on the oscilloscope.
  • Verify voltage values at 1τ, 2τ, and 5τ checkpoints.
