Tutorial & Theory

Binary Addition Principles

Binary Adders are digital circuits that perform arithmetic addition of numbers used in ALUs (Arithmetic Logic Units) of processors.

Half Adder

Adds two single-bit binary numbers ($A$ and $B$). It has two outputs: Sum ($A \oplus B$) and Carry ($A \cdot B$).

Full Adder

Adds three binary digits: $A$, $B$, and an incoming carry bit ($Cin$). Allows cascading multiple adders for multi-bit arithmetic.

Experiment Objectives

  • Verify Sum and Carry outputs for all 4 half adder combinations.
  • Switch to Full Adder mode and test 8 carry-in vectors.
  • Analyze arithmetic carry propagation delay.
