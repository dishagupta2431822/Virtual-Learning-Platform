Tutorial & Theory

Interferometry & Coherence

The Michelson Interferometer splits a beam of light into two paths, reflects them off mirrors, and recombines them to produce interference fringes.

Interference Condition

When movable mirror M2 moves by distance $d$, the total path difference changes by $2d$. Each complete fringe shift corresponds to a mirror displacement of $\lambda / 2$.

Wavelength Determination

By counting fringe transitions ($N$) for a known displacement $d$, laser wavelength is calculated precisely: $\lambda = \frac{2d}{N}$.

Experiment Objectives

  • Measure the wavelength ($\lambda$) of coherent laser light.
  • Determine micro-displacements of mirror M2 by counting fringe shifts ($N$).
  • Analyze circular interference fringe patterns under path differences.
