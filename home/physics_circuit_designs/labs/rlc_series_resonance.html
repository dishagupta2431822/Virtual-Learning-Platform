Tutorial & Theory

AC Series Resonance

An RLC series circuit enters resonance when inductive reactance ($X_L = 2\pi f L$) exactly cancels capacitive reactance ($X_C = \frac{1}{2\pi f C}$).

Minimum Impedance ($Z = R$)

At resonance, total circuit impedance reaches its absolute minimum value equal solely to resistance $R$, producing maximum AC current.

Quality Factor ($Q$)

The Q-factor ($Q = \frac{X_L}{R}$) measures circuit sharpness and voltage magnification across reactive components.

Experiment Objectives

  • Determine resonant frequency $f_r$ experimentally by finding peak current.
  • Analyze bandwidth ($\Delta f = f_2 - f_1$) and quality factor $Q$.
  • Verify phase angle $\theta = 0^\circ$ at resonance point.
