Tutorial & Theory

Relative Motion & Wave Physics

The Doppler Effect is the change in frequency or wavelength of a wave in relation to an observer who is moving relative to the wave source.

Approaching Source

Wavefronts compress together, decreasing observed wavelength and increasing pitch ($f' > f_0$).

Receding Source

Wavefronts stretch apart, increasing observed wavelength and decreasing pitch ($f' < f_0$).

Experiment Objectives

  • Calculate observed frequency shifts for approaching vs receding sources.
  • Observe wavefront compression and Mach cone formation at Mach 1.0+.
  • Analyze the combined effect of source and observer velocities.
