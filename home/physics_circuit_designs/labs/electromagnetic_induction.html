Tutorial & Theory

Faraday's & Lenz's Law

Faraday's Law of Induction states that any change in the magnetic environment of a coil of wire will cause a voltage (electromotive force or EMF) to be induced in the coil.

Faraday's Equation

$\mathcal{E} = -N \frac{\Delta \Phi_B}{\Delta t}$. Induced EMF is proportional to the number of turns $N$ and the rate of magnetic flux change.

Lenz's Law

The negative sign (-) represents Lenz's Law: the induced current flows in such a direction that its magnetic field opposes the change in magnetic flux that produced it.

Experiment Objectives

  • Generate induced voltage by moving a bar magnet through a wire coil.
  • Verify linear scaling of peak EMF with coil turns ($N$) and speed ($v$).
  • Observe galvanometer current reversals when reversing magnet polarity.
